## What this plan changes about the daily pass

- The cursor advances through 1, then 2, then 3+4 together, then 5 to 11 in order; commits 12, 13, 14 and 7 are carried ahead of it as independent fixes and named in `CLAUDE.md` as such, so the cursor line never claims more than the merges performed and never hides a carry either.
- No golden in `test/test_determinism.c` is exposed by 7, 12, 13 or 14: the corpus has no compound and no mover. Each of those records says so as the uninteresting reason, with a counter on the new branch rather than an argument.
- The upstream series as a whole is not behavior-neutral: `f555ee4` and `79fc559` each moved several of upstream's own goldens, and any SAT hunk ever ported would move every hash in both modes because edge-axis selection feeds `b3MakeFeatureId`.
